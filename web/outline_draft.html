  • Manufacturing → Tobacco: drilling into "Manufacturing" reveals Tobacco Manufacturing accounts for almost half the block; eight tobacco brands held positions across three decades
  • Tobacco's collapse: peaked at 7 simultaneous title sponsors in 1999; EU directive 2005 + FIA voluntary phase-out cleared the field by 2008
  • What replaced tobacco — three eras in sequence:
    • 2007–2015: Telecoms tried and failed (AT&T-Williams, Vodafone-McLaren)
    • 2015–2022: Beverages and motor vehicles consolidate (Red Bull, Martini-Williams, BMW retreats)
    • 2022–2026: Fragmented across software (Oracle, HP, Atlassian), oil & gas (Aramco, Orlen, Petronas), crypto/fintech — no single category replaced tobacco

§ 03 — Where the money comes from

Geographical change of title sponsors

  • Western Europe's dominance (1981–late 1990s): Italian tobacco, French oil, German auto suppliers, British financial institutions — F1 was a structurally European sport
  • Wave 1 post-2000 — Asia Pacific: Petronas (Malaysia), Bridgestone (Japan), Japanese manufacturers and electronics
  • Wave 2 post-2010 — Middle East: Aramco (2021), Orlen (2022), ADNOC (2023) — three state-owned energy companies in three consecutive years; not coincidence
  • chart shows the long arc from European near-monopoly to today's multi-polar distribution (explanation and interviews)
